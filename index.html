Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Kiddo Games Studio ("we", "our", or "us") developed Animal Puzzle Adventure. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile game.

We respect the privacy of children and families and are committed to protecting users' privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Animal Puzzle Adventure is designed to provide an enjoyable animal jigsaw puzzle experience for children.

We do not knowingly collect, request, or store personally identifiable information directly from children through the gameplay features of our app.

The game can be played offline. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Third-party advertising services may process certain device and technical information as necessary to provide and support advertising services. The information handled may depend on the advertising service, device settings, and applicable privacy requirements.

For our child-directed app, we configure AdMob with child-directed treatment and set the maximum ad content rating to G, where supported by the SDK and applicable advertising settings.

We do not intentionally use advertising settings to serve inappropriate advertisements to children.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Data Security and Retention

We do not operate our own user account system or collect personal information through the game's puzzle gameplay.

Any information processed by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies, practices, and retention policies.

4. Children's Privacy - COPPA and GDPR

Animal Puzzle Adventure is designed for children and families. We are committed to complying with applicable children's privacy laws and regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable.

Our privacy practices include the following commitments:

  • We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly from children through gameplay.
  • We configure advertising services with child-directed treatment where applicable.
  • We set the maximum ad content rating to G for our child-directed advertising configuration.
  • We aim to follow applicable Google Play Families Policy requirements and children's privacy protections.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about children's privacy in our app, please contact us.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our app may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. We encourage users and parents to review those policies to understand how information may be handled.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage parents and users to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
